Meet Me

✦

I’m Kayla, a technical Product Manager with an engineer’s mindset and a creator’s curiosity. I build products where thoughtful technology meets human experience.

I started my career writing code, learning how systems behave, break, and scale. That foundation still shapes how I think today, not just about what we build, but how and why it works. Over time, I found myself drawn beyond implementation and into vision: asking deeper questions about users, crafting solutions, and shaping products end-to-end. Product management became the natural intersection of everything I love: logic, creativity, and impact.

My work sits at the crossroads of AI, web development, and user experience. I’ve helped bring patented, AI-driven products to life, built applications from the ground up, and designed experiences that feel intuitive, useful, and even a little delightful. I’m especially inspired by the everyday (small frictions and overlooked moments) and I love turning those into elegant, meaningful solutions.

Outside of work, creativity continues to be a throughline in my life. You’ll usually find me traveling, practicing yoga or meditation, or making something with my hands like painting, pottery, or anything a little messy and imaginative. I believe the best products come from people who stay curious, observant, and creatively engaged with the world around them.
